How XP works

Scrobbles: XP from playcount milestones

Achievements: 150 XP per unlocked badge

Artists: XP from unique artist milestones

Account Age: 150 XP for 10+ year accounts

Profile Complete: 150 XP for name, image, country

Total XP determines your level (1–10)

Roast Me: Your Consent

We'll send the following public profile data to OpenRouter to generate your roast. Nothing private is shared. Those following items are sent:

  • Your Last.fm username
  • Your country
  • Total scrobbles, top artist, and unique artist count
  • Account age, average daily listens, and friend count
  • Your unlocked achievements

Roasts are generated by a third-party model (OpenRouter). The data above is sent over the network, you may choose to cancel if you are uncomfortable with this.

Release Notes

What's new in tastecheck.me?

Every update related to shipped feature, fix, and redesign

September 2026
Feature

Choose your roast intensity

Pick Casual or Savage before roasting your profile: light-hearted or no-mercy roasting, your call.

Feature

Shareable roast cards

Turn any roast result into a downloadable, shareable image card: your avatar, level, top stats, and the roast quote itself, ready to send to a friend.

Fix

More accurate daily achievements

We now check more of today's scrobbles when looking at daily achievements, so "Having Fun with Yourself?" can actually unlock instead of getting stuck below the threshold.

Design

One-tap search on the homepage

Added a Send button to the homepage search bar, so submitting your username no longer requires pressing Enter.

August 2026
Feature

Social sharing previews

Links to tastecheck.me now show a proper title, description, and preview image when shared on social platforms.

Feature

Rebrand to tastecheck.me

The project was renamed from "lastfm-achievements" to tastecheck.me, with rewritten copy and documentation to match.

Fix

Navigation and toast cleanup

Fixed inconsistent nav spacing and a handful of toast-notification bugs across the site.

Fix

Favicon fixes

Favicons now load correctly in every environment instead of breaking due to relative paths.

Design

One accent system, site-wide

Landing, dashboard, compare, guide, and about pages were all redesigned to share a single warm paper background with one ink + coral accent system.

Design

Unified achievement badge colors

Achievement badges and accent colors were brought under one consistent theme instead of several mismatched hues.

July 2026
Feature

Compare & joint roasts

Added a two-user comparison page, complete with a shared "joint roast" generated from both listening histories.

Feature

AI Roast

Introduced the AI-generated roast: a witty, judgmental take on your listening habits, generated from your actual stats.

Feature

Mobile-friendly dashboard

Redesigned the dashboard navigation for mobile, and added health/readiness checks behind the scenes for uptime monitoring.

Fix

Navigation & button consistency

Fixed a broken redirect from the guide page and evened out inconsistent button styling.

Design

Achievement detail dialogs

Locked achievements now show a teaser instead of staying blank, and tapping any badge opens a detail popup (a bottom sheet on mobile).

May 2026
Feature

Initial launch

tastecheck.me went live: enter a Last.fm username and get back achievements, XP levels, and listening stats.

Privacy Policy

Updated on: July 22, 2026

What we track

We collect anonymous analytics events when you use this app: page views, profile searches, button clicks, and achievement dialog opens.

What we don't track

We do NOT collect your Last.fm username, personal data, browsing history, or any information that could identify you. All events are anonymous.

How data is stored

Analytics data is sent to Mixpanel, our analytics provider. Data is stored on Mixpanel's servers with anonymized identifiers.

How long is it kept

Data is retained according to Mixpanel's default retention policy. You can contact us to request deletion.

Third-party links

This app links to Last.fm and OpenRouter. Their privacy practices are outside our control.

Contact

For privacy questions, reach out via the project's GitHub repository.

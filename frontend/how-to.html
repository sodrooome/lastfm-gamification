Scrobbles XP

Each tier is cumulative — reaching a higher threshold automatically grants XP from all lower tiers.

Playcount Threshold XP Awarded
1 5
100 20
1,000 40
10,000 80
100,000 180
1,000,000 460
Example: A user with 15,000 scrobbles earns: 5 (tier 1) + 20 (tier 2) + 40 (tier 3) + 80 (tier 4) = 145 XP

Unique Artists XP

Cumulative milestones based on the number of unique artists in your listening history.

Unique Artists Threshold XP Awarded
50 30
100 60
500 90
1,000 120
Example: A user with 750 unique artists earns: 30 (tier 1) + 60 (tier 2) + 90 (tier 3) = 180 XP

Achievements XP

Each unlocked achievement grants a flat 150 XP. With 10 lifetime achievements available, that's up to 1,500 XP total.

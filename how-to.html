How XP is Calculated

Your total XP comes from four sources. Each tier below is cumulative.

Scrobbles XP

Reaching a higher threshold grants all lower tiers automatically.

Playcount XP
1 +5
100 +20
1,000 +40
10,000 +80
100,000 +180
1,000,000 +460

Example: 15,000 scrobbles → 5 + 20 + 40 + 80 = 145 XP

Unique Artists XP

Based on distinct artists in your listening history.

Unique Artists XP
50 +30
100 +60
500 +90
1,000 +120

Example: 750 unique artists → 30 + 60 + 90 = 180 XP

Achievements XP

Each unlocked achievement grants a flat 150 XP. With 12 lifetime achievements available, that's up to 1,800 XP from achievements alone.

Bonus XP

+150 XP for accounts registered 10+ years ago.

+150 XP for a complete profile (real name, avatar, and country set).
